Area, Perimeter and Volume

How much paint, how many floor tiles, how much soil for the planter? These three ideas answer all of them.

~10 min3 lessonsProject calculator5-question quiz

01

Perimeter: the distance around

Perimeter is the total length of the edges, like the fence around a garden. Just add up the sides.

Example · a 5 m × 3 m garden
  1. 5 + 3 + 5 + 3
  2. 16 m of fencing

For a circle, the distance around is the circumference: π × diameter, about 3.14 × the distance across.

02

Area: the space inside

Area is measured in square units (m², ft²): how many 1 × 1 squares would cover the shape.

ShapeArea
Rectanglelength × width
Triangle½ × base × height
Circleπ × radius² (radius = half the width)
Example · paint for a 4 m × 2.5 m wall, one door
  1. Wall: 4 × 2.5 = 10 m²
  2. Door (about 2 m × 0.8 m): 1.6 m²
  3. Paint: 10 − 1.6 = 8.4 m², × 2 coats = 16.8 m²
  4. Check the tin: if 1 litre covers 12 m², you need about 1.4 litres

Units trap: 1 m² is not 100 cm². It's 100 cm × 100 cm = 10,000 cm².

03

Volume: how much fits inside

Volume is measured in cubic units (m³, cm³) or litres. 1 litre = 1,000 cm³, and 1 m³ = 1,000 litres.

ShapeVolume
Box (cuboid)length × width × height
Cylinderπ × radius² × height
Example · soil for a 1.2 m × 0.4 m planter, 0.3 m deep
  1. 1.2 × 0.4 × 0.3 = 0.144 m³
  2. × 1,000
  3. 144 litres of soil
