Mental Math Tricks

Work out bills, tips and totals in your head faster than you can find your phone's calculator. A handful of tricks covers most everyday sums.

01

Round, then fix it

Awkward numbers are easier when you round them to something friendly, do the sum, then correct.

Example · 47 + 38
  1. Round 38 up to 40: 47 + 40 = 87
  2. You added 2 too many, so take 2 off
  3. 85
Example · 6 × $19.99
  1. Treat it as 6 × $20 = $120
  2. Take off 6 × 1¢ = 6¢
  3. $119.94
Example · 83 − 29
  1. 83 − 30 = 53
  2. You took 1 too many, so add it back
  3. 54
02

Split hard multiplications

Break one number into tens and ones, multiply each part, then add.

Example · 7 × 48
  1. 7 × 40 = 280
  2. 7 × 8 = 56
  3. 280 + 56
  4. 336

Or halve and double: halve one number, double the other, and the answer doesn't change.

Example · 16 × 25
  1. Halve 16, double 25: 8 × 50
  2. Again: 4 × 100
  3. 400

04

Squaring numbers ending in 5

Take the first digit(s), multiply by the next number up, and stick 25 on the end.

Example · 35²
  1. 3 × 4 = 12
  2. Add 25 to the end
  3. 1,225
Example · 85²
  1. 8 × 9 = 72
  2. Add 25 to the end
  3. 7,225
