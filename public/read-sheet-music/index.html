How to Read Sheet Music

Those dots and lines are easier than they look. Four short lessons cover what you need to start reading simple music for piano, voice or almost any instrument.

01

The staff and the clefs

Music is written on a staff: five lines and the four spaces between them. Higher on the staff means a higher note.

The clef at the start tells you which notes the lines stand for. The treble clef (𝄞) is for higher sounds, like the right hand on piano, violin, flute or most singing. The bass clef (𝄢) is for lower sounds, like the left hand on piano, cello or bass guitar.

Notes are named with letters A to G, then the pattern repeats.

02

Naming the notes

Lines (bottom to top)Spaces (bottom to top)
Treble 𝄞E G B D F: “Every Good Boy Does Fine”F A C E: spells “FACE”
Bass 𝄢G B D F A: “Good Boys Do Fine Always”A C E G: “All Cows Eat Grass”

Middle C sits on a short extra line (a ledger line) just below the treble staff, or just above the bass staff. It's near the middle of a piano keyboard.

03

How long each note lasts

The note's shape tells you how long to hold it, counted in beats (a steady pulse, like a foot tap):

NoteLooks likeBeats in 4/4 time
WholeHollow oval, no stem4
HalfHollow oval with stem2
QuarterFilled oval with stem1
EighthFilled oval, stem and flag (or joined by a beam)½

A dot after a note adds half its length again: a dotted half note lasts 3 beats. Rests are silences with the same lengths.

04

Time signatures, sharps and flats

The time signature is the two stacked numbers at the start. The top number is beats per bar; the bottom says which note gets one beat (4 = a quarter note).

  • 4/4: four beats per bar, the most common. Count “1 2 3 4”.
  • 3/4: three beats per bar, the feel of a waltz. “1 2 3, 1 2 3”.

A sharp ♯ raises a note slightly (one half step, the very next key on a piano). A flat ♭ lowers it. A natural ♮ cancels them. Sharps or flats at the very start (the key signature) apply to that note all the way through.

Practice idea: pick a simple song you already know, find its sheet music, and clap the rhythm before playing the notes.
